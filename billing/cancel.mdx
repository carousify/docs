You can cancel a monthly or yearly subscription yourself at any time. You keep your plan until the end of the period you've paid for.

## Cancel your subscription

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Billing & Subscription">
    Go to **Settings › Billing & Payments** in the left sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Cancel">
    **Cancel** sits next to **Change Plan** on your current plan card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tell us why">
    Pick a reason in the **Cancel Subscription** dialog, add comments if you like, and click **Continue**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    You may be offered a discount to stay. To go ahead, click **No thanks, I still want to cancel**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  **Cancel** is only available for monthly and yearly subscriptions paid through Dodo Payments. Lifetime plans and plans activated with a license key don't renew, so there's nothing to cancel.
</Note>

## What happens next

What you see depends on how close you are to your renewal date:

| When you cancel | Status | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| More than 7 days before renewal | **Pending Cancellation** | Nothing changes yet. Carousify cancels the subscription a few days before the renewal date, so you aren't charged again. Until then you can click **Reactivate** to keep it. |
| Within 7 days of renewal | **Cancelled** | The subscription is cancelled right away, so it won't renew. |

Either way, the plan card shows **Expires on** with the date your paid period ends, and you keep every feature of your plan until then.

## After your plan ends

On the expiry date your account moves to the **Free** plan:

- Your workspaces, drafts, carousels, templates, brand kits, and connected accounts stay where they are
- You can't publish or schedule new posts
- Team members can't switch into workspaces you own until you're on a paid plan again
- Plan features such as approval, custom roles, link tracking, webhooks, and whitelabel stop working. A whitelabel custom domain is released 7 days after the plan ends; your brand settings are kept — see [Whitelabel overview](/whitelabel/overview#if-your-plan-changes)

To pick up where you left off, choose a plan on the **Billing & Subscription** page.

## Failed payments

If a renewal payment fails, your plan shows **Pending Expire**. Update your card with **Manage Payments** — Carousify keeps your plan for a few days while the payment is retried before moving you to the Free plan.

## Refunds

Every plan comes with a 7-day, no-questions-asked refund on your first payment. Renewals and upgrades aren't refunded. To ask for a refund, [contact support](https://carousify.com/contact-us/).

## Delete your account instead

Cancelling keeps your account and content. To remove everything, cancel first and then [delete your account](/billing/account-security#delete-your-account).
