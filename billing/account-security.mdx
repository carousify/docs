Your Carousify account is personal — it's the login you use for every workspace you own or belong to. Security settings live under **Settings › Password & Security** in the left sidebar (`/user-settings/security`).

## Password

If you signed up with email, change your password here: enter your current password, then the new one twice.

If you signed up with Google or LinkedIn, you don't have a password yet. Setting one lets you sign in with email and password too, and it's required before you can turn on two-factor authentication.

Passwords must be 8–32 characters and include an uppercase letter, a number, and a special character.

## Two-factor authentication

Two-factor authentication (2FA) asks for a one-time code after you sign in. Once it's on, the code is required for **every** sign-in method — email and password, **Sign in with LinkedIn**, **Sign in with Google**, Google One Tap, and Facebook — including on whitelabeled domains.

<Note>
  You need a password on your account before you can set up 2FA. If you only use Google or LinkedIn sign-in, [set a password](#password) first.
</Note>

You can turn on either method, or both.

### Authenticator app

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Set Up next to Authenticator App">
    The **Setup Authenticator App** dialog opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scan the QR code">
    Use an authenticator app such as Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator, or Authy. If you can't scan, type the code shown under **Or enter this code manually**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the 6-digit code and click Verify & Enable">
    The Two-Factor Authentication card now shows **Enabled**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Text message (SMS)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Set Up next to Text Message (SMS)">
    The **Setup SMS Authentication** dialog opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your phone number">
    Pick your country code, enter the number, and click **Send Verification Code**. You can send the code up to 4 times.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the code and click Verify & Enable">
    The code is valid for 10 minutes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Signing in with 2FA on

After your password or social sign-in, Carousify shows a **Two factor authentication** screen. Enter the code from your authenticator app or the text message. If you set up both methods, the app code is asked for first — use **Get a code on phone instead** to switch to SMS, or **Use authenticator instead** to switch back.

### Turn 2FA off

Click **Revoke** next to the method you want to remove and confirm with your password.

<Warning>
  Keep access to your authenticator app or phone. If you lose both, contact [Support](https://carousify.com/contact-us/) to regain access.
</Warning>

## Actions that need your password

Two actions can't be undone easily, so Carousify asks for your password before running them (accounts without a password skip this check):

- **Transferring workspace ownership** — see [Transfer ownership](/workspaces/invite-team#transfer-ownership)
- **Deleting your account** — see below

## Delete your account

Open **Settings** and the **Delete Account** tab.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Cancel your subscription first">
    Carousify won't delete an account with an active paid subscription. [Cancel it](/billing/cancel) first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tell us why (optional) and confirm">
    Pick a reason, then enter your password. If your account has no password, type `DELETE` instead.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Delete Account">
    You're signed out right away.
  </Step>
</Steps>

About 7 days later the account is permanently deleted, along with:

- Every workspace you own and everything in it — posts, carousels, templates, brand kits, uploads, connected accounts, and analytics
- Your membership in other people's workspaces
- Your whitelabel custom domains, which are released so they stop pointing at Carousify

<Warning>
  Deletion can't be undone. If other people rely on a workspace you own, [transfer ownership](/workspaces/invite-team#transfer-ownership) to them before you delete your account.
</Warning>
