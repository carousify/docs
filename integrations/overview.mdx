Carousify covers the social side of your stack — drafting, scheduling, analytics, and approvals for LinkedIn and Instagram. Integrations are how it talks to everything else: planning content in Notion, getting post alerts in Slack, and forwarding events through Zapier into anything you already automate.

You don't need any of these to use Carousify. Pick the ones that match a real friction in your workflow.

## Where to find it

In the left sidebar, open **Manage Workspace › Integrations**.

<Note>
  This page is for third-party tools only. LinkedIn and Instagram accounts live under **Manage Workspace › Social Integrations** — see [Connect your LinkedIn account](/getting-started/connect-linkedin) and [Connect Instagram](/getting-started/connect-instagram).
</Note>

Each card shows an **Active** or **Inactive** status. On an inactive card, click **Connect** to start the setup flow. Once a tool is connected, the card shows:

- **View** — the current settings: the Notion column mapping, the Slack webhook, or the Zapier API key (only the last four characters are shown).
- **Disconnect** — removes the integration after you confirm.

To change an integration's settings — a different Notion database, Slack channel, or Zapier key — disconnect it and connect it again.

<Note>
  Integrations are scoped to a single workspace. If you run a workspace per client, connect Notion, Slack, or Zapier inside each workspace separately — settings don't carry across.
</Note>

<Tip>
  If your plan doesn't include integrations, **Connect** opens an upgrade prompt. If your role doesn't allow managing integrations, Carousify shows a permission message — ask the workspace owner or an admin.
</Tip>

## Don't see what you need?

The integration list will keep growing. If a tool your team relies on isn't here yet, drop a note via [Support](https://carousify.com/contact-us/) — popular requests move up the roadmap. In the meantime, [webhooks](/webhooks/overview) and the [MCP server](/mcp/overview) cover most custom workflows without waiting for a built-in integration.
