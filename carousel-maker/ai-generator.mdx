The **AI Generator** designs a whole carousel from a prompt. Unlike [Smart Carousel](/carousel-maker/create#generate-with-smart-carousel), which only rewrites the text on slides you already have, the AI Generator creates the slides themselves — layout, colours, typography, and copy — and opens the result in the Carousel Maker, where you can keep editing it like any other deck.

<Note>
  The AI Generator is in **Beta**. Results vary from run to run — generate again or refine with the AI Assistant if the first design isn't right.
</Note>

## Open the AI Generator

In the left sidebar, go to **Carousels › AI Generator**. You land on the **Generate a carousel** page.

## Generate a carousel

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose how to start">
    - **From scratch** — the AI designs a new layout for your topic.
    - **From a template** — click **Choose a template or draft as reference**, pick one from the **Templates** or **Drafts** tab, and click **Use as reference**. The AI follows that design's structure and style and writes new content for your topic.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe the carousel">
    Type what the carousel should cover in the prompt box. The more specific you are, the better the result — *5 money habits to build wealth in your 20s, one habit per slide, end with a question to the reader* lands closer than *money tips*.

    Stuck for a topic? Click one of the **Example prompts** below the box, or **Shuffle** for new ones.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Attach a source (optional)">
    Click **Attach** to base the carousel on a document — PDF, PowerPoint (PPT, PPTX), Word (DOC, DOCX), plain text, or Markdown, up to 10 MB.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the size and slide count">
    Choose the canvas size — **Standard** (56:71), **Portrait** (4:5), **Feed** (3:4), or **Square** (1:1) — and the number of slides, from 3 to 20. Default is 8.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set advanced options (optional)">
    | Option | Choices |
    | --- | --- |
    | **Amount of text per slide** | **Minimal**, **Concise** (default), **Detailed**, or **Extensive**. |
    | **Images** | **No images** (default), **Image placeholders** — empty frames you fill in yourself — or **Stock photos**. |
    | **Tone** | **Professional** (default), **Casual**, **Bold**, **Storytelling**, or **Educational**. |
    | **Outro call-to-action** | What the last slide asks for: **Follow** (default), **Save**, **Visit link**, **Product**, or **None**. |
    | **Slide numbering** | Show a number on each slide. On by default. |
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Generate">
    Generation can take up to two minutes. When it's done, the carousel opens in the Carousel Maker with your workspace's [Brand Kit](/carousel-maker/brand-kit) — name, handle, and logo — already applied.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Refine it with the AI Assistant

After generating, an **AI Assistant** panel sits in the corner of the Carousel Maker. Click it and describe what to change in plain language — for example:

- *Make the colours warmer*
- *Shorten the text on every slide*
- *Add a slide with a real-world example after slide 3*
- *Use a bolder font for the headings*

The assistant updates the whole carousel and replies when it's done. Keep going until you're happy, then edit any slide by hand in the **Content** and **Global** tabs as usual. Close the panel when you no longer need it.

<Tip>
  Use the AI Assistant for changes across the deck — colours, tone, structure — and make small fixes to individual slides by hand. It's faster than describing a one-word edit.
</Tip>

The carousel isn't saved until you click **Save Draft** (`⌘ S`) in the editor. From there, [download or post it](/carousel-maker/export) like any other carousel.

<Note>
  Generating and refining carousels uses your workspace's AI usage. If your workspace uses its own AI key, generations run on that key instead. See [Plans](/billing/plans).
</Note>

## AI Generator or Smart Carousel?

| | AI Generator | Smart Carousel |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Creates the design | Yes — layout, colours, fonts, and copy | No — keeps your existing slides' design |
| Needs existing slides | No | Yes, at least 3 |
| Refine afterwards | Chat with the AI Assistant | Generate again |
| Where | **Carousels › AI Generator** | **Smart Carousel** in the editor's top bar |

<Tip>
  Working with Claude, ChatGPT, or another AI assistant? It can design new carousel layouts for you too, through the [Carousify MCP server](/mcp/overview).
</Tip>
