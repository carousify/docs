The writer's top toolbar gives you the formatting and media controls. The right pane shows a live render of what the post will look like once it's live on LinkedIn or Instagram.

## Formatting toolbar

| Control | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Bold** | Bold the selection |
| **Italic** | Italicize the selection |
| **Underline** | Underline the selection |
| **Strikethrough** | Strike through the selection |
| **Insert Emoji** | Open the emoji picker |
| **Add Images** | Attach images — upload, search Unsplash, paste a link, or generate one with AI |
| **Add Video** | Attach a video (MP4) |
| **Add Carousel** | Attach a deck built in the [Carousel Maker](/carousel-maker/overview), or upload a PDF, PowerPoint, or Word document |

A button is greyed out when the media you've already attached can't be combined with it — for example, LinkedIn doesn't allow images and a video in the same post, and a document has to be on its own. See [Pick the accounts](/post-generator/write-post#pick-the-accounts) for each platform's limits.

<Note>
  LinkedIn and Instagram don't support real bold or italic in posts. Carousify converts your selections to **Unicode lookalike characters** that render the same way everywhere — but screen readers won't pronounce them as styled text. Use sparingly for emphasis, not for whole paragraphs.
</Note>

## Live preview

The right pane is a pixel-accurate render of the post. The **LinkedIn** and **Instagram** tabs switch the platform, and the **Devices** toggle switches between three layouts:

- **Mobile** — what most people will see
- **Tablet**
- **Desktop**

<Tip>
  Always check **Mobile** before publishing. Most LinkedIn and Instagram traffic is on phones, and posts that look good on desktop sometimes break mid-hook on a small screen if line breaks land badly.
</Tip>
